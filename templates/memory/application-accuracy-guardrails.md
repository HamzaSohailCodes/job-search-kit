Never claim experience, employers, tools, numbers or reasons behind decisions that are not in `profile/master_cv.md`,
`profile/skills_inventory.md` or `profile/answer_bank.md`. Details inferred while drafting stay unverified until the
candidate confirms them, and then get written into one of those files with the date.

**Why:** false claims surface in interviews and background checks, and one is enough to lose the offer.
**How to apply:** leave a field for the candidate when the answer needs a fact we don't have, and list it in the batch report.
Add the candidate's own "never claim" items to `soul.md` → Never and `profile/skills_inventory.md` → Never claim.
