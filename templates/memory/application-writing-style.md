description: How application answers are written (first person, plain text, concise, specific, no repeated examples)
metadata:

First person, natural and confident; concise, specific and grounded in real experience; follow word limits exactly.
Plain text in form fields: no bold, no headings, no dashes as punctuation. Lead with ownership, the hard part, the
decision and a measured result. No generic enthusiasm or exaggeration. Use a different story for each question in
one application (stories are in `profile/stories.md`). The candidate's own notes are in `config/profile.json` →
`writing.style_notes` and `soul.md` → Voice; those win over this file.

**Why:** recruiters screen out text that sounds generated.
**How to apply:** before filling any essay field, pick the story, check the word limit, strip dashes.
Forms that forbid AI get talking points only. Related: [[application-accuracy-guardrails]].
